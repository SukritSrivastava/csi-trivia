Colour

  • 12 colours. Each one is greyed out once taken, like the felt dolls in Jackbox.
  • When all 12 are gone, a colour can be taken again with a different head.

Head

  • 8 shapes: box, round, antenna, bolts, horns, ears, dome, visor.
  • 12 × 8 = 96 unique bots, which covers the 60–100 player range.

Fonts

  • Typewriter for questions, answers and names.
  • Terminal digits for the numbers, timer and counts, same as A.
